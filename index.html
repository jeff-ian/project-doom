After returning the Infinity Stones, Steve Rogers (Captain America) made a fateful decision: he stayed in the past to live out a full life with Peggy Carter. But altering history and leaving behind a secret legacy carries consequences. In rewriting his own ending, Captain America may have unknowingly fractured the timeline—and set the stage for Doomsday.

X-MEN'S GAMBLE
In a nightmare future, advanced Sentinels have evolved to wipe out nearly all mutants—and anyone carrying the mutant gene. Cornered and facing extinction, the surviving X-Men send Wolverine’s consciousness back to 1973 to alter the past. The gamble works, but breaking their intended destiny came with a price: a permanent rift in the timeline that was never meant to exist.

FANTASTIC FOUR: FAMILIAR GROUND
While other heroes triggered multiverse chaos through time travel, the Fantastic Four's relationship with Doom is grounded in their past. Reed Richards and Doom share an ancient rivalry, and their histories have always been intertwined. Yet, why they stand opposed on the road to Doomsday remains an open question. With subtle teases like young Franklin Richards interacting directly with Doom, it is clear: the Fantastic Four aren't dealing with a complete stranger, but an obscure, familiar presence whose true motives are still in the dark.
